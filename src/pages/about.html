About Battram Associates

Independent expertise,
rooted in experience.

Battram Associates is an independent, building surveying and design-led practice based in Tring, with the capability and local experience to cover the northern Home Counties. Our Principal, Mark Battram, has over 35 years' post-qualification experience, and all our reports are prepared by a Chartered Building Surveyor.

Why choose us

Our surveyors write reports in plain English with common sense advice, and take the time to make sure you understand the technical issues. Our designers manage the complete design and build process, from planning and listed building matters through to instruction on site. We understand that quality, cost and time are the key components of any successful project, and we have long experience of sourcing the right craftspeople through tendering.

Our projects have been featured in national magazines and on Channel 4's Homes by the Sea with Charlie Luxton.

Meet the team

A small practice means you deal directly with the people doing the work.

Mark Battram

Principal · MRICS · RICS Conservation Diploma

Chartered in 1987 and conservation accredited since 1995, Mark worked on large national contracts for Wetherspoon, Vodafone and private London landlords before founding the practice around 25 years ago to focus on the Home Counties. He has seen too many aesthetically good designs let down by poor use of materials, and it shapes how the practice works. Outside work: marine life and endurance cycling.

Alison Ingles

Surveyor

Alison came to surveying from a law degree and a teaching background, completing the RICS-accredited Postgraduate Diploma in Building Surveying at the University of Salford and now nearing the end of her APC for Chartered status. Her work covers party wall matters, heritage statements, building surveys, quinquennial specifications and planning, with a particular interest in the conservation of historic churches using traditional techniques.

Amelia Edwards

Architectural Designer · BA (Hons) Architecture · RIBA Part 1

Amelia joined in 2012 as an Architectural Technician and has since moved into architectural design on the practice's more complex projects, achieving a First Class BA (Hons) in Architecture and RIBA Part 1 along the way. Over a decade of practical industry experience gives her a strong grasp of both the creative and technical sides of design, and collaboration and communication are central to how she works.

Ruth Mitchell

Surveyor · MRICS · RICS Conservation Accredited · IHBC

Ruth joined in 2025. A Building Surveying graduate of Robert Gordon University, she became RICS Chartered in 2017, conservation accredited in 2020, was shortlisted for RICS Building Surveyor of the Year in 2023 and became a full member of the IHBC in 2024. Her focus is heritage and listed building conservation, from building surveys and heritage statements to quinquennial inspections and project management, driven by a real enthusiasm for historic properties.

From diagnosing existing buildings and conserving historic fabric to creating new spaces, our work is grounded in practical knowledge, careful thinking and a close understanding of how buildings are made.
