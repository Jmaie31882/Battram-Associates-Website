Our team

Mark Battram MRICS

Director

Mark trained as an Architectural Technician before completing a Building Surveying honours degree at De Montfort University, qualifying MRICS in 1987 and gaining the RICS Conservation Diploma in 1995. After years running large national roll-out contracts for clients such as Wetherspoon and Vodafone, he founded the practice around 25 years ago and chose to concentrate on London and the Home Counties. Thirty-five years of practical experience, and a conviction that good design depends on the right materials, underpin everything the practice does.

Our Principal initially trained as an Architectural Technician, but soon after Mark completed a Building Surveying honours degree at De Montfort University. Mark became MRICS qualified in 1987, and then moved onto achieving an RICS Conservation Diploma in 1995, with the intent to be able to work with our Heritage buildings.

Mark's portfolio of work experience previously focussed on large national roll-out contracts in the residential, telecommunication, retail and brewery industries, for clients including: Wetherspoon, overseeing Architects and Contractors in nationwide multi-million pound fit outs; Vodafone, designing and instigating store fit outs nationwide and particularly involved in Listed Building and Conservation Area sites; and private landlords, maintaining and adapting large scale listed buildings in Kensington, Fulham and Chelsea.

Circa 25 years ago Mark founded a sole trader business, after gleaning a wealth of surveying, design and project management skill sets. Over time, the focus of the business shifted to further projects in London and locally in the Home Counties, reining back on the need to work nationwide. This was a decision made to concentrate locally and it has led to a good network of consultants and contractors that all form part of the "team" in client projects.

Mark has over the years seen aesthetically good design by others that has been fundamentally flawed by poor use of materials, leading to extensive maintenance and costs. He has therefore developed a passion for good design via the practical use of the best materials. This philosophy applies to new vernacular style bespoke homes and extensions, as well as particularly to the conservation of our heritage assets. Mark's wealth of practical knowledge gleaned over 35 years adds exceptional value to each project, no matter how small or large the engagement.

Outside of work Mark's interests include immersing in marine life and knowledge, as well as being a keen endurance cyclist, completing the Tour de France route for charity in 2014.

Ruth joined Battram Associates in 2025 as a Building Surveyor, bringing a strong passion for heritage and conservation. She graduated from Robert Gordon University, Aberdeen, with a degree in Building Surveying in 2012 and became a chartered member of the Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors (RICS) in 2017. Pursuing her interest in the historic environment, Ruth achieved RICS conservation accreditation in 2020. In 2023, she was shortlisted for RICS Building Surveyor of the Year, and in 2024 she became a full member of the Institute of Historic Building Conservation (IHBC).

Since joining the team, Ruth has focused on the conservation and management of heritage assets and listed buildings. She is particularly committed to sensitive interventions that respect the character and significance of each property, ensuring appropriate materials and techniques are used. Her work encompasses building surveys, conservation advice, heritage statements, quinquennial inspections, and project management.

Ruth's approach is shaped by a genuine enthusiasm for historic properties and a belief that careful, thoughtful conservation can extend the life and beauty of these important buildings. Outside of work, Ruth is a keen runner and enjoys exploring the Chilterns and surrounding countryside.

With an education in Law and a background in teaching, Alison joined Battram Associates in 2019.

Alison completed a Post Graduate Diploma in Building Surveying (RICS accredited) with the University of Salford and is nearing completion of the Assessment of Professional Competence (APC) to become Chartered with the Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors.

Alison's law degree provides a strong foundation in legal aspects related to property, construction and contracts. Alison has wide ranging experience including party wall matters, heritage statements, building surveys, quinquennial specifications and planning issues.

Amelia joined Battram Associates in 2012 as an Architectural Technician, developing a strong foundation in technical design, construction methods and the delivery of detailed architectural drawing packages.
