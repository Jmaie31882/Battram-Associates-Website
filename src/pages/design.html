About The Design Studio

Architecture
rooted in place.

We design homes and spaces that enhance the way people live, combining architectural creativity with practical expertise.

From extensions and renovations to new homes, we take a considered, collaborative approach.

Our services
  • 01

    Feasibility Studies and Measured Surveys

    Exact measurements of the existing plot, elevations and floor plans, and an honest appraisal of what planning policy, site constraints and budget allow before formal design begins.

  • 02

    Concept and Spatial Design

    We get to know how you live and what you want, then turn that brief into sketch schemes and tangible options, with practical considerations such as structure and services built in from the start.

  • 03

    Extensions

    Rear, side and two-storey extensions, orangeries and loft rooms, designed to sit naturally with the existing building, including listed buildings and homes in conservation areas.

  • 04

    Renovations

    Reimagining existing space to unlock its potential, from reconfiguration to whole-house refurbishment.

  • 05

    Planning and Technical Design

    Developed design and Design and Access Statements, planning and listed building applications, then Building Regulations drawings and detailed specifications for accurate tendering.

  • 06

    New Bespoke Homes

    Individual houses from feasibility through planning, technical design and contract administration on site.
