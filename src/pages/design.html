What we do

Because the studio sits alongside our surveying and heritage work, designs begin with a real understanding of the existing building. That means fewer surprises on site, better detailing and schemes that planners and conservation officers can support.

  • Extensions & Renovations

    Rear, side and wrap-around extensions, loft conversions and whole-house refurbishments.

  • Reconfiguration

    Rethinking how existing space works before adding more of it.

  • New Homes

    One-off houses from feasibility and site appraisal through to completion.

  • Planning Applications

    Householder, full and listed building consent applications, including pre-application advice.

  • Technical Packages

    Building Regulations drawings, construction details and tender packages.

  • 3D Visualisation

    Models and visuals so you can see and refine the design before it is built.
