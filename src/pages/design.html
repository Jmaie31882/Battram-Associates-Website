What we do

Because the studio sits alongside our surveying and heritage work, designs begin with a real understanding of the existing building and its materials. That means fewer surprises on site, better detailing, and schemes that planners and conservation officers can support. Our projects have been featured in national magazines and on Channel 4's Homes by the Sea with Charlie Luxton.

  • Alterations and Extensions

    From orangeries and two-storey extensions to whole-house renovations and loft rooms, including work to listed buildings and homes in conservation areas.

  • New Bespoke Dwellings

    One-off houses from planning through to completion, from Arts and Crafts and Georgian-inspired homes to timber-framed coastal houses.

  • Reconfiguration and Renovation

    Rethinking how existing space works before adding more of it, with practical considerations such as structure and services built in from the start.

  • Planning and Listed Building Consent

    Preparing and directing applications, including Design and Access Statements and liaison with the local authority.

  • Technical Packages

    Building Regulations drawings, detailed specifications and tender packages, working alongside structural engineers.

  • 3D Visualisation

    Models and visuals so you can see and refine the design before it is built.

From first sketch
to final fix.

We can take a project all the way through, or step in for just the stages you need.

  1. Measured survey

    Exact measurements of the plot, elevations and floor plans, with levels where needed, as the basis for all design work.

  2. Feasibility

    Weighing planning policy, site constraints and cost to set out the options and their relative merits before any formal design.

  3. Brief and sketch schemes

    We get to know how you live and what you want, then turn that into tangible options, with realistic cost projections from the outset.

  4. Developed design and planning

    Finalising the design and materials, CAD plans and sections, and directing the planning application.

  5. Tender

    Technical drawings and highly detailed specifications for Building Control and contractor pricing, which keeps tender returns accurate and reduces cost fluctuation during the build.

  6. Contract administration

    Advising on the right form of contract, then regular valuations, progress and quality checks on site.
