About Battram Associates

Independent expertise,
rooted in experience.

Based in Tring and working across the northern Home Counties, Battram Associates is an independent, building surveying and design-led practice. All our reports are prepared by a Chartered Building Surveyor, and our Principal has over 35 years' post-qualification experience. We work with homeowners, custodians of historic buildings and churches, and private clients from first advice through design and construction.
