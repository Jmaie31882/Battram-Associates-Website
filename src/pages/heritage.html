Overview

Sensitive advice for historic buildings.

We work with homeowners, institutions and developers to care for historic buildings, helping to preserve their significance while adapting them for modern use. Our heritage expertise combines a deep understanding of traditional construction with practical, cost-effective advice.

Our heritage services
  • {{> icons/column}}

    Listed Building Repairs

    Timely intervention and regular maintenance are key to sustaining the value of a listed building. We assess issues and specify repairs using traditional methods and sympathetic techniques.

  • {{> icons/house}}

    Church Restoration

    Advice to churches on re-ordering and long-term restoration projects, grounded in traditional materials and construction techniques.

  • {{> icons/pencil}}

    Historic Building Recording

    Records from Level 1 (photographs and notes) to Level 4 (comprehensive analytical record), in line with Historic England guidance, often required by Listed Building Consent.

  • {{> icons/doc}}

    Heritage Statements

    Researched statements for Listed Building Consent applications, describing a building's significance and the impact of proposals.

  • {{> icons/clipboard}}

    Quinquennial Reports

    Five-yearly inspections of church buildings. We are approved by the Dioceses of St Albans and Oxford and currently look after thirteen churches.

  • {{> icons/cog}}

    Maintenance Programmes and Investigative Reports

    Forecasting budgets, schedules of work for tendering, liaison with conservation officers and oversight of the works.

Our approach

Understanding the past.
Protecting the future.

We take the time to understand the history, significance and fabric of each building, combining technical knowledge with a thoughtful, pragmatic approach. Our aim is to protect what makes historic buildings special, while helping them to remain practical, usable and relevant for the years ahead.
