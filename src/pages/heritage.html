What we do

Historic buildings need a different approach: an understanding of traditional materials and construction, of how they have changed over time, and of the planning framework that protects them. We help owners and custodians care for their buildings and make changes that respect their significance.

  • Listed Building Repairs

    Specification and oversight of repairs using appropriate materials and methods.

  • Heritage Statements

    Assessments of significance and impact to support planning and listed building consent applications.

  • Historic Building Recording

    Measured and photographic recording to Historic England levels.

  • Quinquennial Inspections

    Five-yearly inspection reports for churches and other ecclesiastical buildings.

  • Church Restoration

    Repair and conservation projects for places of worship, from fabric repairs to reordering.

  • Heritage Advice

    Early advice on what is possible before you commit to a scheme.

Repair, not replace.

Our starting point is always to retain as much historic fabric as possible. Where change is needed, we aim for work that is honest, reversible where it can be, and of a quality that will itself be valued in a hundred years.
