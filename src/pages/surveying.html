Overview

Independent surveying advice for every stage.

Our surveying services give homeowners, property owners and institutions the information and confidence they need to make informed decisions. From condition surveys to project management, we provide clear, practical advice based on detailed knowledge of existing buildings.

Our surveying services
  • {{> icons/house}}

    Level 3 RICS Surveys

    We offer RICS Level 3 surveys as we believe this level of inspection offers the best value for money. Our thorough inspections and detailed reports provide in-depth advice on condition, allowing you to make an informed purchase and plan for maintenance or upgrades.

    We also offer CAA authorised drone surveys.

  • {{> icons/search}}

    Specific Defect Reports

    Our inspection and analysis identifies specific issues such as damp, timber decay and cracking. We diagnose causes, comment on further risks and recommend practical repair methods and solutions.

  • {{> icons/doc}}

    Party Wall Matters

    We provide detailed advice on notifiable works, serve notices on behalf of building owners and form Party Wall Awards. We act as impartial professionals under the Party Wall etc. Act 1996, appointed by building owners, adjoining owners and as agreed surveyors.

  • {{> icons/cog}}

    Contract Administration

    Our Contract Administration service maintains continuity from the Design Studio to completion. Our role for clients includes the formation of contracts, site progress visits, and the instruction of variations and valuations. Our practical support helps to ensure construction meets the required standards and contract terms are met.

  • {{> icons/clipboard}}

    Condition Reports

    We provide detailed reports on property condition to inform future maintenance schedules. By providing timescales and budgets for necessary works, we support owners and custodians to plan for the future.

  • {{> icons/pencil}}

    Specifications and Schedules of Work

    Clear, concise technical specifications for contractors, supporting decision making and robust, compliant repairs.

Our approach

We take the time to understand each building and its context, combining technical expertise with a thoughtful, pragmatic approach. Our goal is to provide clear advice that protects the character of buildings and helps our clients plan with confidence.
