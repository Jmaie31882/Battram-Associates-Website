What we do

Our surveying work is grounded in a practical understanding of how buildings are put together, how they age and how they fail. We work on everything from family homes to Grade I listed buildings and churches, and our advice is tailored to each client's needs. Please note that we do not carry out valuations.

  • RICS Level 3 Building Surveys

    Recommended for older properties, those which have been altered, and if you are considering any major works. An in-depth analysis of a property's condition with clear advice on defects, repairs needed and ongoing maintenance, written to be of practical help when purchasing.

  • Specific Defect Reports

    A focused inspection of a particular problem, such as damp or unexpected cracking to walls, with recommendations for remedial action. Where necessary we will advise if further investigation is needed, such as instructing a Structural Engineer.

  • Condition Reports

    Identifying the condition of a building to advise on its long-term needs. Our reports feed into planned maintenance and the budgeting of future works, particularly for Grade I listed buildings, churches, listed modern estates and community facilities.

  • Party Wall Matters

    If you or a neighbour are carrying out works affecting a party wall, including foundations and loft conversions, the Party Wall etc. Act 1996 must be followed. We act as surveyor for the building or adjoining owner, or as agreed surveyor, communicating impartially with all parties and producing a detailed Award specifying the work, when and how it is to be done.

  • Specifications and Schedules of Work

    For any defect, alteration or scheme of work we produce a technical specification for use by a contractor. Our understanding of materials, technical requirements and best practice ensures a robust solution that complies with current legislation.

  • Drone Inspections

    Carried out with a CAA-authorised operator, drone inspections allow close inspection of areas with limited or difficult access. Repeated over a series of months they build a valuable picture of how an issue is progressing. Clients include churches and Grade I listed buildings.

How it works

Every instruction starts with a conversation about the building and what you need to know. We agree the scope, fee and timescale before any site visit.

  1. Enquiry

    Tell us about the property and your plans.

  2. Proposal

    A clear scope and fee, usually within a working day.

  3. Inspection

    A thorough visit by a Chartered Building Surveyor.

  4. Report

    A clearly written report, followed by a call to talk it through.
