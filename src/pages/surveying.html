What we do

Our surveying work is grounded in a practical understanding of how buildings are put together, how they age and how they fail. Reports are written in plain English, with photographs and clear priorities, so you know what matters and what can wait.

  • RICS Level 3 Building Surveys

    The most thorough pre-purchase survey, suited to older, larger or altered properties.

  • Specific Defect Reports

    Focused investigation of a single issue such as damp, cracking, timber decay or roof problems.

  • Party Wall Matters

    Notices, awards and schedules of condition under the Party Wall etc. Act 1996.

  • Condition Reports

    Concise records of a building's condition for landlords, purchasers and lenders.

  • Technical Specifications

    Specifications and schedules of work for repair and refurbishment projects.

  • Project Management

    Tendering, contract administration and site inspections through to completion.

How it works

Every instruction starts with a conversation about the building and what you need to know. We then agree the scope, fee and timescale before any site visit.

  1. Enquiry

    Tell us about the property and your plans.

  2. Proposal

    A fixed fee and clear scope, usually within a working day.

  3. Inspection

    A thorough visit by a qualified surveyor.

  4. Report

    A clear written report, followed by a call to talk it through.
